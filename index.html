About the Council

Telangana State Nurses & Midwives Council

⚖️

Statutory Body

Constituted under the AP Nurses and Midwives (Extension & Amendment) Act, 1964, adapted to Telangana vide G.O.Ms No. 80, HM & FW (F1) Dept, dated 26.09.2015.

🎯

Our Mission

To prepare safe, competent, and compassionate nursing professionals through evidence-based practice, lifelong learning, and autonomous administration.

🏥

Our Mandate

To establish and maintain a uniform standard of nursing education for Nurses, Midwives, ANMs, and Health Visitors across the State of Telangana.

The Telangana State Nurses and Midwives Council (TSNMC) is an Autonomous Statutory Registration body dedicated to regulating and advancing the nursing profession in Telangana. The Council oversees the registration of nurses, approval of nursing institutions, and ensures that education and practice standards remain aligned with national and international benchmarks.

TSNMC is committed to social responsibility, professional integrity, and the continuous development of nursing education — ensuring that every registered professional upholds the highest standards of patient care and public health across the state.
